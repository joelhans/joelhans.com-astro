Another one, and a generous one: <a href="https://independentbookreview.com/2026/07/07/the-bedtime-emptying-of-our-world-by-joel-hans/">a starred review of <span className="[font-variant:small-caps]">The Bedtime Emptying of Our World</span></a> from Melissa Suggitt at *Independent Book Review*.

> *The Bedtime Emptying of Our World* is a cohesive, wildly inventive collection. Each time you think you understand the shape of the book, author Joel Hans pulls the rug out from under you to reveal a strange, beautiful, heartbreaking direction.

And this, which rank *very* highly on the kindest way anyone's described what I try to do with my writing:

> Beneath every magical transformation, impossible creature, and dreamlike landscape is a question about how we love each other, how we lose each other, and how we continue forward anyway.

If you'd like to be pulled in a strange, beautiful, heartbreaking direction (aka, buy a copy), you can do that [from the press](https://www.uapress.com/product/bedtime-emptying-of-our-world/) or [from Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Bedtime-Emptying-Our-World-Stories/dp/0913785695).
